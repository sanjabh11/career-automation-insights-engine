## 3.3 Segment Profiles

### Segment B: B2B Career Coaches (BEACHHEAD)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Firmographics** | Solo career coaches, resume writers, executive coaches, career counselors. 109,200+ coaches globally (ICF 2023), ~15% career-focused = ~16,000 addressable. US + global English. |
| **Behavioral signals** | Already use software tools (Calendly, ChatGPT, CRM, coaching platforms). Active on LinkedIn. Attend ICF conferences. Charge $150-500/session. |
| **Intent signals** | Searching for "career coach tools," "white-label career reports," "AI career assessment tools." Visiting coaching platform sites (Intry, CoachStackHub, Shapeshifter). |
| **Technographics** | Web-based tools, Stripe payments, Google Workspace, LinkedIn. No HRIS integration needed. |
| **Pain signals** | ChatGPT gives generic advice without data credibility. Manual research takes 2-10 hours per client. Need authoritative, source-labeled data to justify premium pricing. Can't produce branded reports efficiently. |
| **Urgency** | Medium — not "board is asking" urgent, but coaches are actively looking for ways to differentiate from free ChatGPT advice and justify their fees. |
| **WTP** | $149/mo validated by Intry and CoachStackHub at same price point. Coaches already pay $20/mo for ChatGPT + $16/mo for Calendly + $29-$99/mo for coaching platforms. |
| **Competition** | LOW for automation-specific white-label reports. General coaching platforms (Shapeshifter, Careerflow, Mentorrd, Intry) exist but none offer O*NET-grounded automation defense reports. |
| **Reachability** | HIGH — LinkedIn outreach, ICF chapter meetings, cold email, coach associations, content marketing on "automation defense for coaches." |
| **Adoption barriers** | LOW — self-serve signup, no integration required, white-label branding is a standard feature coaches expect. |
| **Negative ICP evidence** | Some coaches may be price-sensitive solo operators. Some may not see automation defense as their niche. ICF certification is not required to be a coach, so quality varies. |

## 3.5 Key Insight

**The product is almost perfectly built for B2B career coaches but has never been marketed to them.** The codebase has:
- ✅ White-label counselor report generator
- ✅ O*NET-grounded analysis with source labels
- ✅ Bulk occupation analysis
- ✅ $149/mo Coach Pro tier with real Stripe price IDs
- ✅ Dedicated ForCoachesPage.tsx landing page
- ✅ Credit packs for PAYG ($49-$299)
- ✅ Client management dashboard
- ✅ API access (1K calls/day)

**What's missing:**
- ❌ Distribution (no marketing has been done — $0 revenue, zero customers)
- ❌ Proof (no testimonials, case studies, or demos)
- ❌ Coach-specific onboarding/trial
- ❌ Active outreach to coach community

The gap is **go-to-market execution**, not product capability. The product is built; it needs to be sold.
