## 2.3 Status Quo: How Target Users Currently Solve the Problem

### B2C Knowledge Workers
- **Current behavior**: Google "will AI replace my job" → find WillRobotsTakeMyJob or articles → get a percentage → feel anxious → do nothing actionable
- **Pain point**: Percentage without action plan. "I know I'm at risk but I don't know what to do about it."
- **WTP signal**: Most users expect this for free. 12+ free competitors reinforce this.

### B2B Career Coaches
- **Current behavior**: Use ChatGPT to research automation risk for client sessions. Manually compile notes. Charge $150-500/session for human judgment.
- **Pain point**: ChatGPT gives generic advice without O*NET grounding. No data credibility. Can't produce branded reports. Time-consuming manual research.
- **WTP signal**: Coaches already pay $29-$299/mo for coaching platforms (Intry $99-$299, CoachStackHub $29-$149, Shapeshifter, Careerflow). $149/mo is a validated price point.

### Enterprise HR
- **Current behavior**: Hire consultants at $500K-$2M for workforce AI impact assessments. Or do nothing and hope the board doesn't ask.
- **Pain point**: Consultants are expensive and slow (6 months). Board wants answers now.
- **WTP signal**: $10K-$50K per engagement. But 8+ well-funded competitors already serve this market.

### Veterans
- **Current behavior**: Use O*NET Online Military Crosswalk (free, basic) or VeteranCareerPath.com (free, AI-powered). Transition assistance programs (TAP) are mandatory but generic.
- **Pain point**: Military skills don't translate cleanly to civilian job titles. Need resume help + career direction.
- **WTP signal**: Federally funded programs pay for transition services. Individual veterans have low personal WTP.

## 2.8 Counter-Evidence Pass 1: Is B2C Viable?

**Challenge**: The B2C AI job risk calculator market is structurally challenged.

| Evidence | Source | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer AI market is experiencing a "profound drought" — structural mismatch between LLM inference costs and consumer WTP | AINews (July 2026) | 🔴 Fundamental challenge to B2C model |
| Users expect free AI services (cultural barrier set by Google, social media) | AINews, VaaSBlock | 🔴 Free expectation is cultural, not solvable by product quality |
| 12+ free competitors in AI job risk calculator space | Web research | 🔴 Saturated market with zero pricing power |
| ChatGPT Free is "sufficient for light use" — can answer "will AI replace my job?" | NumberVibe, AI subscription analysis | 🟡 Product must clearly differentiate from free ChatGPT |
| Most AI subscriptions cost $20/mo — product's $29/mo is above anchor | NumberVibe | 🟡 Price above market anchor requires clear value justification |
| Kairo (closest competitor) is free beta with same target audience | Kairo website | 🔴 Direct substitute at $0 |

**Resolution**: B2C is viable ONLY as a freemium acquisition channel, not as primary revenue. The free tier should drive SEO traffic and funnel to B2B coach revenue. The $29/mo Defender tier may convert a small percentage but cannot be the primary revenue driver given market saturation.
