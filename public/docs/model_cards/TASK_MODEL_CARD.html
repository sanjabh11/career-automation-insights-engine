Use for career-coaching, workforce-planning discussion, and prioritizing human review. Do not use as a job-loss prediction, employment decision, salary guarantee, or automated eligibility/ranking system.

Performance and Calibration

The Phase B starter calibration artifact reports ECE 0.235, MAE 23.5 percentage points, and RMSE 26.5 percentage points on 7 source-backed fixture rows. These figures validate the calibration pipeline and disclose mismatch against external anchors; they do not certify production accuracy.

Limitations

  • Current public calibration uses a small source-backed fixture; live expert-label collection remains a manual gate.
  • O*NET/BLS are U.S.-centered; UK/CA/AU localization is a later phase.
  • LLM behavior and labor-market conditions can drift; results require periodic recalibration and human review.
  • Task-level outputs are sensitive to prompt wording, occupation coverage, and source freshness.
