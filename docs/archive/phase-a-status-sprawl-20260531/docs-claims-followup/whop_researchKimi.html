Value Proposition

Core Transformation

The Career Automation Insights Engine transforms a community from a passive content-consumption space to an active, personalized career resilience hub. By offering members a "Geiger counter for automation risk," it quantifies individual vulnerability and provides clear, actionable paths forward, making the community an indispensable resource.

Boost Engagement

Provides deeply personal and urgent reasons for members to interact through AI-driven risk assessments that spark conversation and debate within the community.

Increase Retention

Differentiate Value

Offers proprietary, data-driven tools that address pressing concerns of modern professionals, setting your community apart in the crowded Whop marketplace.
