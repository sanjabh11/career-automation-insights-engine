# APO Outreach Operating Plan

Last updated: May 8, 2026
Primary motion: founder-led LinkedIn and email outreach
Claim standard: proof-tiered pilot outreach only

## Summary

APO should be marketed as an automation-risk forecasting and transition-planning product, not as a broad career-tool bundle.

The active business lane is proof-pack pilot outreach. Every outreach message must anchor on one visible route, one buyer problem, one reviewable artifact, and one explicit claim boundary.

Outreach can start before every product gap is closed, but only under this rule:

> Sell bounded pilot reviews around visible proof packs. Do not claim the product is world-class, production-proven, or broadly ready to sell until the proof gates in `docs/APO_TOP10_RUNTIME_PROOF_CHECKLIST.md` are complete.

## Outreach Readiness Rule

Use these labels in outreach planning:

| Label | Meaning | Outreach Use |
|---|---|---|
| `Lead` | Route and artifact are visible enough for a controlled review. | Use as the main reason to contact a prospect. |
| `Attach` | Useful trust or context surface, but not the first pitch. | Attach after interest, before a serious pilot. |
| `Pilot-only` | Good buyer problem, but implementation proof is incomplete. | Offer a bounded review, not a production claim. |
| `Park` | Too weak, too broad, or too likely to overclaim. | Do not use in first-wave outreach. |

## Daily Workflow

1. Pick one buyer lane for the day:
   - Career coach / resume writer
   - Outplacement consultant
   - Workforce board / community college career services
   - Utility HR / L&D / workforce planning
   - Boutique HR consultant
2. Pick one proof pack from the route matrix.
3. Check the proof status before sending:
   - Route opens.
   - Artifact is visible or explainable.
   - Caveats are clear.
   - No unsupported `LIVE` or production claim is needed.
4. Spend 10 minutes on LinkedIn notifications and replies.
5. Spend 20 minutes commenting on buyer-relevant posts with useful, non-promotional observations.
6. Send 5 to 10 personalized connection requests or DMs.
7. Log every real send immediately.

## Weekly Workflow

1. Review replies by proof pack and buyer lane.
2. Count:
   - connection requests sent
   - accepts
   - replies
   - sample-report requests
   - demo calls booked
   - pilot conversations opened
3. Identify the strongest and weakest proof packs.
4. Tighten copy only where the problem statement, artifact, or claim boundary is unclear.
5. Update product proof blockers in `docs/APO_TOP10_RUNTIME_PROOF_CHECKLIST.md` if outreach exposes a blocker.

## Message Rules

- Lead with a buyer problem, not a feature list.
- Offer a sample report or 20-minute proof-pack review.
- Mention the specific route only when it helps the buyer understand what can be reviewed.
- Keep the first DM short enough to answer with "yes", "not now", or a named occupation/use case.
- Do not send more than two follow-ups without a reply.
- Do not use automation tools for first-wave outreach.

## Stop Conditions

Do not send if the message implies:

- deployed customers or production pilots that are not proven
- guaranteed career outcomes
- guaranteed placement, salary increase, or job safety
- Lightcast equivalence
- regulator, university, or government approval
- O*NET alone proves automation risk
- GPU superiority or proprietary model superiority
- SCADA, power-flow, ADMS, operational technology, or energy-load forecasting capability
- privacy, compliance, or deletion guarantees that are not implemented and verified

## Manual Send Limits

- Maximum 10 new LinkedIn connection requests per weekday until reply quality is known.
- Maximum 5 DMs per weekday in the first two weeks.
- Maximum 1 message per firm per day.
- Maximum 2 contacts per firm across an active batch.
- Stop a sequence after 2 follow-ups without a reply.

- Build a list of 50 career coaches and 20 workforce/career-service contacts.

- Send 10 coach messages using `coach_resilience_report`.

Week 2:

- Send 15 more coach messages.
- Send 5 workforce-board messages using `bridge_role_pathway`.
- Record the most common objection and route blocker.

Week 3:

- Add 20 utility HR/L&D contacts.
- Send 5 utility workforce audit pilot messages using `utility_workforce_audit`.
- Attach responsible AI/methods proof only after interest.

Week 4:

- Review reply quality by proof pack.
- Keep the best two proof packs.
- Park any proof pack that creates overclaim risk or requires unproven runtime behavior.
