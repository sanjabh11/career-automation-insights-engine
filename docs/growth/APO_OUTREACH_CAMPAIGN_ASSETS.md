# APO Outreach Campaign Assets

Last updated: May 8, 2026

## Campaign Thesis

APO should be sold as automation-risk forecasting plus transition-planning proof packs, not as a broad career dashboard.

The strongest campaign promise is a reviewable artifact:

- career resilience report
- resume AI-proof score
- bridge-role transition plan
- skill-adjacency map
- utility workforce automation audit
- responsible AI and methods appendix

The first outreach wave should be manual, founder-led, and proof-tiered. Broad public launch language should wait until runtime proof, provenance, reporting, and buyer workflow gaps are closed.

## Lead List Criteria

Career coach first wave:

- Active LinkedIn posting in the last 60 days.
- Mentions career transition, executive career coaching, resume writing, outplacement, or LinkedIn profile optimization.
- Works with mid-career professionals, tech workers, executives, students, or displaced workers.
- Avoid prospects who only sell generic resume templates and show no advisory positioning.

Utilities workforce first wave:

- Utility, grid modernization, energy workforce, L&D, HR, or workforce planning role.
- Publicly discusses workforce, skills, cybersecurity, grid modernization, field operations, or training.
- Prefer utilities, utility associations, workforce boards, and consultants over engineering-only teams.
- Avoid implying operational grid analytics or load forecasting.

HR consultant first wave:

- Fractional CHRO, workforce transformation, people analytics, L&D strategy, or HR consulting title.
- Serves mid-market clients that likely cannot buy enterprise workforce-intelligence tools.
- Uses advisory artifacts, audits, reports, or workshops in their services.

## Copy Blocks For Lead-Now Proof Packs

### Coach White-Label Career Resilience Report

Subject: White-label career resilience report for {Practice}

APO turns automation exposure, task drivers, bridge roles, and skill gaps into a client-ready report. The goal is to help coaches discuss AI career risk with evidence instead of generic advice.

Use this proof pack for career coaches, resume writers, outplacement consultants, and career strategists.

### Resume AI-Proof Score

Subject: Is this resume signaling replaceable work?

The resume analyzer flags automation-prone phrasing and suggests stronger positioning around judgment, strategy, accountability, and human-context work.

Use this proof pack for B2C content, coach discovery, and outplacement conversations.

### Bridge-Role Transition Pathway

Subject: Bridge roles for AI-disrupted occupations

The bridge-role workflow reframes career transition from unrealistic jumps to feasible paths through adjacent roles, missing skills, and learning next steps.

Use this proof pack for coaches, workforce boards, community colleges, and career services.

### Skill Adjacency Graph

Subject: Visual skill pivots, not generic job lists

The skill-adjacency graph shows adjacent skills and career-neighborhood logic that a static occupation page cannot reveal.

Use this proof pack as a visual demo asset after a buyer understands the problem.

### Utility Workforce Automation Audit

Subject: Utility workforce automation audit pilot

The utility workforce audit frames automation exposure, grid-modernization skills, and reskilling ROI for utility teams.

Use pilot-only language until CSV persistence, department rollups, ROI, and executive report proof are complete.

### Responsible AI And Methods Appendix

Subject: Methods and evidence appendix

Responsible AI, validation, source provenance, and known limitations should be attached to serious B2B conversations. They are trust features, not back-office documentation.

## Objections And Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is this just another resume AI tool?" | "No. Resume analysis is one entry point. The differentiated workflow is automation exposure -> task drivers -> bridge roles -> skill gaps -> report." |
| "Can O*NET prove automation risk?" | "No. O*NET provides structured occupation, task, and skill data. APO adds its own scoring and AI synthesis, with source caveats." |
| "Is this like Lightcast?" | "It is not a Lightcast replacement. It is a focused automation-risk and transition-planning layer for coaches and workforce teams that need a smaller proof-pack workflow." |
| "Can it integrate with our HRIS?" | "Treat the current workforce package as a bounded pilot unless the required import, persistence, and reporting proof has been completed." |
| "Does this guarantee safe careers?" | "No. It supports planning by identifying exposure, transferable skills, and transition options. It is not a guarantee of employment or safety." |

## 30-Day Campaign Sprint

Days 1-7:

- Finalize proof-pack screenshots and sample artifacts.
- Build 50 career coach prospects.
- Publish 2 founder posts.
- Send 10 coach DMs.

Days 8-14:

- Build 30 more coach/outplacement prospects.
- Send 15 additional coach DMs.
- Send 5 career-services messages.
- Record objections.

Days 15-21:

- Build 20 utility workforce prospects.
- Send 5 utility pilot messages.
- Prepare a utility role-list sample using anonymized roles.

Days 22-30:

- Review response quality.
- Keep the top two message variants.
- Park any proof pack that creates product proof pressure.
- Convert interested prospects into 14-day pilot scopes.
