## Safety Reminders

1. **Never exceed 15 connection requests/day** — LinkedIn flags accounts at ~20+
2. **2-5 minute delays** between all actions (connections, messages, likes)
3. **Engage before connecting** — like 1-2 of their posts first, leave a thoughtful comment
4. **Stop on ANY warning** — LinkedIn's "you're reaching the weekly limit" is a hard stop
5. **Weekend pause** — no outreach Saturday/Sunday (coaches don't check LinkedIn)
6. **Personalize EVERYTHING** — generic messages get flagged and ignored
7. **Value-first always** — offer the free tool/report BEFORE mentioning pricing
8. **Track everything** — update the tracking sheet after every session

---

*This playbook is designed for a solo founder doing 45 minutes/day of LinkedIn outreach. Scale with caution — quality > quantity. One good conversation with a coach who has 50 clients is worth more than 100 cold connections.*
